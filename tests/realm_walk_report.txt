Judgement calls for Dan (not changed)
==============================================================================================================
1. Traps vs your HP. A trap costs at most 21 rooms in routing, whatever it does; the Hedge Maze (17/28xx) has 400-700
   damage traps and the walk goes through them (sometimes it's the only way out). On the practice server a 715-HP
   level 55 hung up at 1 HP twice. Weigh traps against your HP / rest to full before a big one?
2. Damaging rooms vs your HP. Volcano tunnels (magma heat 30-60 a room), the Quagmire (bog poison), rivers without a
   raft: long stretches are walked without resting first and end in "Hung up (HP below 25%)". Rest before a known
   damaging stretch, or route round when it's not much longer?
3. Doors you can't open aren't checked when planning. A '[61 picklocks/strength]' door with strength 60 is planned
   through; the walk goes 100+ rooms there and stops ("the door north is locked", 14/332). door_doable is only used
   for key doors.
4. Lever tours are costed at 12 rooms. The 6/861 four-lever puzzle is a 150-room tour and the Treetops one ~45, so a
   Go to can pick a lever way that's really longer than a plain one. Cost the tour by its real length?
5. Dark-Elf Castle gate (8/559 S, 1000 picklocks) only opens from inside ('turn wheel' in 8/560): Go to the castle
   gatehouse now gets to the moat (with the Fungus Tunnel fix) and waits 2 minutes at the gate, then stops.
6. Kingsport is an island on MME's map: the only ways off are the 2,000,000-copper secure passage boats and a level-60
   portal. With the default avoid areas, unattended walks from Kingsport to the Lost City take ~1000 rooms (the 300-
   room way crosses the Negative Power Plane). Inside Kingsport and round it (Crownlands, Albion River) all 90+
   walks were clean in every round.
7. Portals / holes guarded by a monster ('nomonsters' in MME: the Deranged Priest's 'go hole', the master assassin's
   'push panel'): with combat off the walk tries 8 times and stops ("isn't getting anywhere"); the planner doesn't
   know the monster has to die first. With combat on it fights and goes.
8. Map gaps, not MudPilot: Bank of Delanon (5/245) has no way out on MME's map but a level-75 portal; the Volcano's
   Fiery Lair needs the chiseled and red rock keys (quest items); Tidepool from Silvermere without GuildmasterQuest is
   only through Black Wasteland (avoided).
9. The boss-dead change (#6 above) trusts the Bosses-tab clock (MME regen time or your own figure). Veto if you'd
   rather stop for every boss room regardless.
10. Party: each follower needs their own golden idol for the catacombs teleport (16/2273 E); the practice server is
   one character, so the party side wasn't walked.
11. keyed_route ('as if you had every key', the last fallback) can still plan through an item nobody can get; the main
   plan no longer does. Only matters when every other plan fails.
